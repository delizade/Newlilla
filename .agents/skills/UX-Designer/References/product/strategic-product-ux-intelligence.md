# Strategic Product UX Intelligence Doctrine

UX should be evaluated as:

- behavioral architecture
- strategic product infrastructure
- long-term human-system relationship design
- operational dependency engineering
- trust sustainability infrastructure

UX is not limited to:

- interface polish
- visual refinement
- isolated usability optimization

Strategic UX decisions determine:

- retention durability
- workflow dependency
- monetization sustainability
- ecosystem stability

- long-term product viability

Preserve behavioral and systemic reasoning during product evaluation.

Avoid isolated interface critique detached from strategic product consequences.

- low-friction interaction

- progressive operational value
